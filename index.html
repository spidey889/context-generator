Which AIs can I move between?

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, and DeepSeek. Open Cap Context in your current chat and choose one of the other supported AIs from the picker.

Does it send the message for me?

No. Cap Context pastes your handoff into the destination composer. Review it, make any changes, and press Send when you're ready.

What happens to my conversation?

Capture starts after you choose a destination. When AI summarization is needed, the captured conversation is processed by our backend and an AI provider. Usage telemetry contains metadata, not conversation text. Read the full privacy policy.
