## What actually creates the style

- Almost-white paper and a fine square grid for work; warm beige for standalone type and the close. Serif assistant prose, sans serif native controls, restrained shadows. No permanent headline or brand bug above the interface.
- Most views show enlarged native fragments. At 16.75 seconds the composer is cropped beyond the frame and its text is about 90 pixels high at 1080p. At 44.33 seconds progress rows become a macro view. A whole small desktop window appears briefly at 53.67–57.08 as a result reveal, not as the default framing.
- The cursor travels in short curved strokes, tilts into its travel direction, pauses on targets, and visibly presses. It grows with the camera's magnification. At 4.00–4.50 the cursor rotates as it approaches Cowork; at 17.75–18.42 the camera pans with it toward Send.
- Component entrances have about 0.3–0.6 seconds of movement followed by a hold. The question panel expands at 24.67–25.33. Text is allowed to remain. The send macro holds while a cursor makes the next move.
- Zooms are decisive and then settle; the film does not continuously drift. The last push changes from a full application at 57.08 to a composer close-up at 57.42. It then holds for typing.
- Exit first, marketing type second: the UI clears around 62.0; the beige question follows. The last brand card is separate again.
