# Claude Cowork reference — second-cut motion study

The owner's local 68.63-second Cowork film was studied as a motion reference. No source footage, graphics, sound or music appears in this cut. The earlier full study remains with the retained first Kyoto film at `../brag-output-2026-09-29-153657/reference-study.md`.

I revisited dense frame samples around 3–5, 15.75–18.42, 43–45.67, 47.5–50.17, 52–54.67, 56.75–59.42 and 60.5–63.17 seconds. These are the specific choices carried into this edit:

| Reference motion | Cap Context adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| Isolate a single native control; let the cursor arrive, pause, press, then reveal its state. | The Cap Context orb and ChatGPT destination tile each receive target-led pushes; the tile's actual hover and selected states are shown. |
| Jump from a recognisable whole app to a very large composer or progress detail, then settle. | The Claude Send, picker tile, progress stage and ChatGPT Send have distinct camera beats, with no continuous drift. |
| Keep progress visibly attached to the working interface before a macro close-up. | The production handoff card is composited inside the staged Claude chat, with the conversation and limit still visible. The camera pushes into the Summarizing stage and pulls back before completion. |
| Exit work before separate campaign typography. | The ChatGPT answer leaves the frame before the message card, then a separate Cap Context lockup closes the film. |

The second cut is retimed to 52 seconds from a fresh 62-second Kyoto composition: faster target-to-action moves, but readable holds on the limit, selection, transfer and answer. The destination change is an app slide, not a crossfade of two legible chats. The host chats are authored examples; the Cap Context orb, picker, hover/selected tile and handoff markup/styles come from the current production content script.

The local reference's audio was evaluated through waveform and short-time energy in the first study. I could not audition it in this environment and do not claim to have listened to it. This cut uses a new original 100 BPM D-major felt-upright motif, a light pulse and action-synchronized Foley. No reference audio is reused.
