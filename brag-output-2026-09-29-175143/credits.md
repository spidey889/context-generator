The film's Kyoto conversation, staging, camera choreography, typography treatment, score arrangement and synthesized Foley were authored for Cap Context. No footage, graphics, score or effects from the owner's Claude Cowork reference were reused.

The upright-piano instrument uses three soft recordings from [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), released under [CC0-1.0](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/blob/master/LICENSE). The exact sample URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in `work/audio-manifest.json`; `work/score.py` generates the original composition and effects.

The Cap Context icon, typography, orb, destination picker, hover and selected states, and handoff card originate from this project's production extension UI. Their capture provenance is in `work/native/ui.json`. The card's backdrop blur and shadow are suppressed only in the film to prevent a rectangular compositor artifact over the chat; its structure and progress states are retained. Claude and ChatGPT identify the illustrated source and destination. Their surrounding chat shells and conversation are staged examples, not private-account captures. The displayed Claude reset time and transfer timing are illustrative, not a live latency claim. Both ChatGPT Send clicks are user actions.
